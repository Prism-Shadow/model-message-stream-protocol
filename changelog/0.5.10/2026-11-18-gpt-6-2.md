## What changed

- `openai-official` served `gpt-6.2-astra` and `gpt-6.2-sol` as GPT-6 models, and `openai-responses` served `openai/gpt-6.2-astra` and `openai/gpt-6.2-sol` on OpenRouter through its `gpt` plugin: `ThinkingLevel.NONE` ran at `low`, since both models refuse the `none` and `minimal` efforts, `prompt_caching` `enhance` sent `prompt_cache_options: {"ttl": "12h"}` on `openai-official`, and an image above 30,000 patches went out at `detail: "high"`.
- The registry listed the four entries, Text and Image in, Text out, with a 1,050,000-token context: GPT-6.2 Astra at $10 prompt (the 30-minute cache-write rate), $0.40 cached and $40 response per 1M tokens, and GPT-6.2 Sol at $2.50, $0.10 and $10, on OpenAI and on OpenRouter alike. The registry holds 368 entries.
- The shared test catalog tested `gpt-6.2-sol` on `openai-official` in place of `gpt-6.1-sol`, and the playground's model menu opened on `gpt-6.2-sol` in place of `gpt-6.1-sol`.
- `llmsdk_docs/gpt6/` added the GPT-6.2 model pages and refreshed the GPT-6 pages and guides from developers.openai.com.
