# Kimi K3.1 Pro

- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `moonshot_official`, `registry`, `ci`, `docs`, `integration`
- **PR:** [#294](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/model-message-stream-protocol/pull/294)

[中文版](2026-10-23-kimi-k3-1-pro.zh.md)

## What changed

- `kimi-k3.1-pro` and `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed`: `MoonshotOfficialClient` served them in Python and TypeScript with Kimi K3's request contract.
- `thinking_level: none`: on the K3 series, `kimi-k3` included, `MoonshotOfficialClient` sent `reasoning_effort: "none"`, where it had sent `"low"`.
- `prompt_caching: enhance`: on the K3 series, `MoonshotOfficialClient` sent `prompt_cache_options: {"mode": "implicit", "ttl": "1h"}`, where it had raised `UnsupportedParameterError`. `disable` on every Kimi model and `enhance` on Kimi K2 still raised, with the message `Kimi does not support this prompt_caching (disable has no equivalent; enhance needs Kimi K3).`
- Registry: registered `kimi-k3.1-pro` and `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed` on Moonshot, `moonshotai/kimi-k3.1-pro` on OpenRouter and `kimi-k3.1-pro` on TokenDance, and corrected the output price of `moonshotai/kimi-k3` on OpenRouter from 11.936 to 10.8 USD.
- `.github/ci/models.json`: the `moonshot-official` group tested `kimi-k3.1-pro` and `moonshotai/kimi-k3.1-pro` in place of `kimi-k3` and `moonshotai/kimi-k3`.
- `test_thinking_level_mapping.py` and `thinking-level-mapping.test.ts` expected `"none"` for `kimi-k3` at `NONE` and gained rows for `kimi-k3.1-pro` (`NONE`, `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `XHIGH`) and `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed` (`MAX`).
- The configuration page named Kimi K3 and K3.1 Pro among the models that take `"none"` as it is and gave their `thinking_level`, `tool_choice` and `prompt_caching` rows; the models page listed `kimi-k3.1-pro` and `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed` under `moonshot-official`; the README's Kimi row named K3.1 Pro, with `kimi-k3.1-pro` as its example.
- Both `skills/*/reference/models.md` listed `kimi-k3.1-pro` and `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed` on Moonshot, `moonshotai/kimi-k3.1-pro` on OpenRouter and `kimi-k3.1-pro` on TokenDance; the dev skill's example of a model that cannot turn thinking off became MiniMax-M3.1-Flash in place of Kimi K3.
- The playground's Kimi option became `kimi-k3.1-pro` (Kimi K3.1 Pro).
- Added the `llmsdk_docs/kimi_k3_1/` snapshot and refreshed `llmsdk_docs/kimi_k3/`.

## Configuration behavior

| `UniConfig` | K3 series on `moonshot-official` (`kimi-k3`, `kimi-k3.1-pro`, `kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed`) |
| --- | --- |
| `thinking_level: none` | `reasoning_effort: "none"` |
| `thinking_level: low` | `"low"` |
| `thinking_level: medium` / `high` | `"high"` |
| `thinking_level: xhigh` / `max` | `"max"` |
| `tool_choice: "auto"` / `"none"` / `"required"` | the same value |
| `tool_choice` with tool names | `UnsupportedParameterError` |
| `prompt_caching` unset / `enable` | nothing |
| `prompt_caching: enhance` | `prompt_cache_options: {"mode": "implicit", "ttl": "1h"}` |
| `prompt_caching: disable` | `UnsupportedParameterError` |
