# GPT-6.1 Terra, with allowed-tools lists, oversize images and the 12-hour cache on GPT-6

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `openai_official`, `openai_responses`, `utils`, `registry`, `tests`

[中文版](2026-11-02-gpt-6-1-terra.zh.md)

## What changed

- `gpt-6.1-terra` was registered for OpenAI on `openai-official`, and `openai/gpt-6.1-terra` for OpenRouter on `openai-responses`, in both packages. It was added to the shared model catalog, the OpenRouter case marked slow, and its documentation was snapshotted in `llmsdk_docs/gpt6/`.
- `ThinkingLevel.NONE` went out as `reasoning.effort: "none"` on GPT-6.1 Terra in `openai-official` and `openai-responses`; GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol kept `low`.
- `tool_choice` as a list of names went out from `openai-official` and `openai-responses` with `type: "allowed_tools"` beside `mode: "required"` and the tools. `openai-chat` was left as it was.
- `openai-official`, `openai-responses` and `openai-chat` sent `detail: "high"` on an image above 30,000 patches for GPT-6, as for GPT-5.6.
- `prompt_caching: "enhance"` on `openai-official` sent `prompt_cache_options: {"ttl": "12h"}` for GPT-5.6 and GPT-6. `disable`, and `enhance` on earlier GPT models, raised `UnsupportedParameterError` with a new message; `openai-responses` and `openai-chat` still raised on both.
- `llmsdk_docs/openai_responses/docs/openai-responses-create.md` and both fast-mode snapshots were refreshed from developers.openai.com; `llmsdk_docs/gpt6/` gained the pricing page and the image input guide.

## Configuration behavior

| Key | Client and model | Sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `thinking_level: "none"` | `openai-official`, `openai-responses`: GPT-6.1 Terra | `reasoning.effort: "none"` |
| `tool_choice: [names]` | `openai-official`, `openai-responses` | `{"type": "allowed_tools", "mode": "required", "tools": [{"type": "function", "name": ...}]}` |
| `prompt_caching: "enhance"` | `openai-official`: GPT-5.6, GPT-6 | `prompt_cache_options: {"ttl": "12h"}` |
| an image above 30,000 patches | `openai-official`, `openai-responses`, `openai-chat`: GPT-5.6, GPT-6 | `detail: "high"` |

A 12-hour cache write bills at twice the 30-minute write rate; cache writes count in `prompt_tokens`.
