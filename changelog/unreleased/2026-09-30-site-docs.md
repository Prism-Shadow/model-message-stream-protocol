## What changed

- A new page, Design philosophy, explains the two layers of state every MMSP stream carries (`.delta` / `.done` around each item, `delta` / `stop` around the message), what they buy an agent, and how the traces serve debugging and recursive self-improvement.
- Every code example opens on TypeScript, and a language a reader picks is kept on the pages read after it.
- The tracer and the playground can be started from a shell in TypeScript as well as in Python.
- The home page's example imports the client and is laid out one field per line; the English slogan turns between "connect" and "observe", and the vendor row ends "and 1000+ models". The recorded streams read "One codebase to call 1000+ models" (同一份代码调用 1000+ 模型).
- The top bar's star count is in the page from the first paint, so the buttons beside it no longer move when it loads.
- The README's tracer screenshot is retaken.
